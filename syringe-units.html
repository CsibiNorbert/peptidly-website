How many units are on an insulin syringe?

A 1 ml insulin syringe is marked in units. Peptidly converts a volume into those marks and shows where to draw to. Follow the steps and the ring on the app screen shows where each part happens.

Amounts on the app screen are blurred on this site. Run the arithmetic with your own numbers.

  1. The syringe is marked in units

    A 1 ml insulin syringe is marked in units, and 100 units = 1 ml. Each unit is a hundredth of a millilitre, so the barrel reads from 0 to 100.

    In the app: the syringe under Draw to.

  2. The app turns a volume into units

    Peptidly divides the dose by the concentration to get a volume, then multiplies by 100. The result is the Draw to figure, labelled IU, with the volume beside it.

    In the app: the Draw to figure.

  3. How full the barrel is

    OF BARREL shows the draw as a share of the syringe. A draw to the 50 mark reads 50%.

    In the app: the Of barrel chip.

  4. Where to find it

    From the Home screen, the calculator button in the header opens it. A compound’s own screen has a calculator button too.

    In the app: the Home header, then the Calculator screen.
