BAC water is bacteriostatic water, used to mix a powdered vial. Units are the marks on a 1 ml insulin syringe; the app shows them as IU. The calculator only does arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It does not recommend a dose or a compound. Either a comma or a dot marks a decimal, so type thousands without a separator.

  2. What you drew comes off the open vial, so the level on screen is what is left inside it.

  3. When what you hold for a cycle will last two weeks or less, Home names it and says roughly how long it has left.

Every site, remembered.

Log a dose and the site goes with it. Next time, Peptidly suggests the next area in your rotation and passes over any you used in the last three days.

  • Front and back, with your left on the left in both views.
  • Six squares on the abdomen for an exact spot.
  • Thighs, glutes and the backs of the arms, plus the shoulders if you switch them on.
  • A rest window from 1 to 14 days, and areas you don't use switched off.

Questions, answered.

What does it cost?

Peptidly is free to start and has no ads. The JSON export of your data is always free.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Peptidly is an Android app on Google Play, and it runs on Android 7.0 and newer.

Does Peptidly give medical advice?

No. It records what you plan and log, and its arithmetic only uses the numbers you enter. It does not recommend compounds. Always consult a qualified clinician before starting, changing or stopping any protocol.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. What you log is saved on your phone first and syncs to your account when you are back online.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes. Sign in on the new phone and your cycles, vials and dose history come with you.

What can it track?

Vials you reconstitute, ready-mixed bottles and nasal sprays, in mcg or mg, with blends, injection sites and notes.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Spanish, Romanian and Italian.

Found a problem, or have an idea?

Use Send feedback in Settings, or email hello@peptidly.co.uk. Every message is read.
