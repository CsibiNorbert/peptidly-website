Reconstitution maths, with your numbers.

Three steps, the same ones the app takes. Change a number in the calculator and each line follows it, so you can check the draw for yourself.

    vial size ÷ BAC water

A milligram is a thousand micrograms. The calculator takes the vial in milligrams and your dose in micrograms.

Peptide calculator questions.

Is this a peptide dosage calculator?

It works out the draw for a dose you enter, and nothing more. It never suggests how much to take, how often or which compound. Always consult a qualified clinician before starting, changing or stopping any protocol.

How many mcg are in a mg?

A thousand: 1 mg = 1000 mcg. That is why the calculator multiplies the concentration by 1000 before dividing your dose by it.

How do I convert mcg to units?

Divide your dose in mcg by the concentration in mcg per ml, then multiply by 100 for a U-100 insulin syringe. The calculator above does it for you and shows each step.

How much is one unit on an insulin syringe?

On a U-100 insulin syringe, one unit is a hundredth of a millilitre: 100 units = 1 ml. The app labels these marks IU.

What is BAC water?

Bacteriostatic water, used to mix a powdered vial. Enter the volume you added; the calculator divides the vial by it to get the concentration.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and nothing you type leaves it. The page sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Like any website it is served by a host, GitHub Pages, which sees the usual request data; the privacy policy has the details.

Is it the same maths as the app?

Yes. This page uses the app's own arithmetic, and it is tested against the app's functions on thousands of combinations of vial, water and dose.

Can I keep the vial I worked out?
