Injection site rotation log

Peptidly records the injection site with every dose, suggests the next area in your rotation and passes over areas still inside the rest window. Follow the steps to see the dose screen and the setting behind it.

The body map is interactive: tap an area to pick it. See it on the home page.

Step by step

  1. Each dose is logged with a site

    On the dose screen the body map shows every area in use, front and back. Tap an area to pick it, and Peptidly records it with the dose.

    In the app: the body map on the dose screen.

  2. The next area is suggested

    Peptidly suggests the next area in your rotation and marks it NEXT. The area picked is named under the map, and a resting area can still be picked, with a warning.

    In the app: the area named under the map.

  3. Resting areas are passed over

    An area used inside the rest window counts as resting, so the suggestion skips it. The line under the map says which areas were skipped and how long ago they were used.

    In the app: the skipped line under the map.

  4. Strict, Loose or Off

    The Rotation rule sets how firm the rotation is. Strict warns in amber about areas still resting, Loose only notes recent use, and Off asks for a pick every time.

    In the app: Settings, Default sites, Rotation rule.

  5. The rest window is a setting

    Rest window runs from 1 to 14 days, and 3 by default. Picking an area inside the window shows a warning.

    In the app: Settings, Default sites, Rest window.
